A little about us

Our Story

Who We Are

Established in 2021, ACH Imports Ltd combines specialist UK fruit farming with more than 100 years of fresh produce supply chain experience. We supply the UK’s retail, wholesale, and food service sectors. By partnering with like-minded growers worldwide, we ensure their produce enters the UK market with the highest level of care—treating their harvest exactly as they would.

What We Do

ACH Imports connects trusted growers, both locally and globally, with UK retailers, wholesalers and food service. We are experts in delivering premium quality fresh produce efficiently and on time, every time.
